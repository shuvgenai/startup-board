# Pitch Deck & Fundraising Materials

Only run on request. Many founders here (no-code/prompt-first, bootstrapped) don't need this — check before producing a full deck outline nobody asked for.

## Pitch deck outline (standard seed-stage shape)

1. **Cover** — name, one-line positioning
2. **Problem** — the pain, grounded in real interview evidence, not a hypothetical
3. **Solution** — the core loop, not every feature
4. **Why now** — what changed that makes this the right time (market, tech, regulation)
5. **Market size** — TAM/SAM/SOM, sourced from `market-research-modes.md` heavy-mode output; mark any unverified figure clearly
6. **Product** — screenshots/demo of the actual MVP, not a mockup presented as finished
7. **Traction** — whatever real evidence exists (interviews done, waitlist size, early usage) — never inflate a small number, frame it honestly
8. **Business model** — from the Lean Canvas revenue streams
9. **Go-to-market** — from `gtm-launch.md`
10. **Competition** — positioning map from `market-research-modes.md`, showing the gap, not claiming "no competitors" (that's a red flag to investors, not a strength)
11. **Team** — why this founder/team specifically
12. **Ask** — amount, use of funds, milestones it unlocks

## One-pager

A condensed single-page version of the above for cold outreach to investors — problem, solution, traction, ask, contact.

## Likely investor objections

Tie back to board seats — an investor is often unconsciously channeling one of them:
- "Have you talked to customers, or just built this?" (Fitzpatrick's lens)
- "What's your one metric right now?" (Croll & Yoskovitz's lens)
- "Why can't [BigCo] just build this?" (Maurya's unfair-advantage question)
- "What's the real market size, bottom-up?" (Christensen/market-sizing lens)

Prep honest answers to these before the deck goes out — a deck that avoids the hard question invites it harder in the room.

## Output format
Deck as a markdown outline (slide-by-slide, bullet content per slide) suitable to hand to a slide tool, or as an actual .pptx via the pptx skill if the founder wants a built deck rather than an outline.
