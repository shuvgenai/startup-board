# Go-to-Market & Launch

Sized for a solo/prompt-first founder — one channel tested properly beats five channels tried shallowly.

## Channel selection (tie to McClure's AARRR)

Ask: at which funnel stage does this idea actually break first (see board-members.md, McClure)? That determines the channel priority:
- Breaks at **Acquisition** (nobody finds it) → content/SEO, communities where the buyer already hangs out, direct outbound (cold email/DM) — pick one, not all three.
- Breaks at **Activation** (they arrive but don't get value fast) → this is a product problem, not a channel problem; fix onboarding before spending on more traffic.
- Breaks at **Retention** → same, fix the product; more acquisition just increases churn volume.
- Breaks at **Referral/Revenue** → the product works; now it's about pricing and word-of-mouth loops.

## Positioning & messaging brief

- One-sentence positioning statement: "For [target customer] who [problem/job], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit], unlike [alternative] which [key gap]."
- 3 messaging pillars (not more) — each backed by something from the Lean Canvas UVP or interview evidence, not invented claims.
- A single primary CTA for the launch (waitlist signup, book a call, free trial) — one, not three competing asks.

## Pricing recommendation

- Anchor to value delivered and the cost of the current workaround (from interview data), not to competitor list price alone.
- For a first launch, recommend a simple 1–2 tier structure over a complex matrix — complexity can come later once there's usage data.
- Flag if pricing is still a pure guess (mark it, don't present it as researched).

## Launch-week checklist (solo-founder scale)

1. Landing page live with the one-sentence positioning and single CTA.
2. Direct outreach to everyone already interviewed — they get first access, not a cold list.
3. One channel post/thread where the target buyer actually is (a specific community/subreddit/LinkedIn niche, not "post everywhere").
4. A feedback capture mechanism from day one (a form, a Slack/Discord, or just "reply to this email") — don't launch without a way to hear back.
5. Set the one metric being watched for week 1 (tie to OMTM, Croll & Yoskovitz) — usually an activation metric this early, not revenue.

## Output format
A short markdown brief covering the four sections above, tailored to the specific idea — not the generic checklist verbatim.

## After launch
