# Founder Intake Questions

Ask in short batches (2–3 at a time), not a single wall of text. Skip anything the founder already answered unprompted. Use tappable options for anything with a short enumerable answer set; free text for anything that needs explanation.

## Batch 1 — The idea itself
1. In one or two sentences, what does the product do?
2. Who is it for — be specific (not "everyone," not "SMBs" — which SMBs, doing what job)?
3. What problem does it solve, and how are people solving that problem *today* without you?

## Batch 2 — Stage and evidence
4. What stage are you at — idea only, prototype/mockup, built but no users, or paying customers?
5. Have you talked to anyone who has this problem? If yes, roughly how many, and were they people you already knew or strangers?
6. What do you already believe strongly, and what are you genuinely unsure about?

## Batch 3 — Model and constraints
7. How would this make money — subscription, one-time, marketplace cut, ads, something else (or not sure yet)?
8. Is this B2B, B2C, or B2B2C?
9. What's your build constraint — solo, no-code/prompt-first, small team, timeline or budget ceiling worth knowing about?

## Optional follow-ups (ask only if relevant)
- Is there an existing tool/workflow (including spreadsheets, manual processes) this would replace?
- Any geography or regulatory constraint (e.g., healthcare, finance, specific country)?
- Do you have a name or working title already?

Once Batch 1–2 are answered, there's enough to convene the board (Phase 1). Batch 3 sharpens the Lean Canvas and research phase but shouldn't block the board discussion from starting.
